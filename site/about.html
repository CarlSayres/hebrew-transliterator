About

I created Hebrew Transliterator to make it easier for teachers, cantors, clergy, and service leaders to prepare song sheets, handouts, and worship materials that help people participate with confidence. I wanted a tool that could quickly turn vocalized Hebrew into clear, readable transliteration without requiring someone to type and format every word by hand.

You can paste vocalized Hebrew directly, or browse and import biblical and liturgical texts from Sefaria. The application preserves the line and paragraph structure of the source text and supports several transliteration styles and pronunciation options. Sefaria requests are made directly from your browser, so imported material remains current.

Most importantly, I wanted the transliteration to pay careful attention to the details of Hebrew pronunciation: sh'va na, kamatz katan, syllable boundaries, word stress, prefixes, and the many exceptions that give Hebrew its proper sound. Those small details matter, especially when transliteration is being used to teach a melody, lead a service, or help someone encounter an unfamiliar prayer for the first time.

My hope is that Hebrew Transliterator makes creating useful materials simpler while giving students and worshippers a more accurate and approachable guide to reading, singing, and praying in Hebrew.
