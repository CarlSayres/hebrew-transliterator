About

Hebrew Transliterator converts vocalized Hebrew into readable Latin characters while preserving line and paragraph structure. It supports several transliteration styles and optional pronunciation settings.

You can paste vocalized Hebrew directly or browse and import biblical and liturgical texts from Sefaria. Sefaria requests are made directly from your browser, so imported material remains current.

The application was created by Cantor Carl Sayres to make Hebrew pronunciation more approachable for learners, worshippers, educators, and clergy.
